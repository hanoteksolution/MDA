# School Phase 6 — fees, shared billing and reconciliation

Date: 2026-09-22. Authorized scope: Phase 1 audit §N row 6, billing entities in §K, accounting integration §I and finance gate §P; fee/accounting cases in the School test matrix. Phase 5 remains verified PASS. No Phase 7 work.

## Delivered

- Campus fee categories and draft/active/closed structures with dated period lines; requested/approved/rejected scholarships and discounts. Discounts use Decimal half-up rounding and net-at-issuance policy. Approved discounts are immutable; later reductions use approved credits.
- Persisted fee previews show every student, payer, gross fee, approved discount and net amount. Issuance validates the saved fingerprint against current enrollment, customer, structure, discount and mappings. Unique enrollment/category/period keys prevent duplicate charges across retries/batches. Invoice, fee assignments and ledger posting commit together.
- Shared `sales.ServiceInvoice` and `ServiceInvoiceLine` provide a non-stock path alongside existing product-backed InvoiceItem rows. School links the shared Invoice, enrollment/family and Customer; guardian identity remains separate. No product, inventory movement, COGS, School payment table or editable family balance is created. Fully waived charges retain an assignment and paid zero invoice, without inventing a zero-value journal.
- Shared configurable payment methods, economic receipts, allocations, credits and refunds. Receipt: Dr cash/bank/mobile mapping, Cr advance liability. Allocation: Dr advance liability, Cr invoice AR. One receipt supports partial allocations across invoices for the same tenant/campus/customer. Funds and invoice balances are bounded under transaction locks; retry keys reject changed payloads.
- Credits target a specific invoice line and approved outstanding amount. Recognized fees use the configured contra-revenue/discount account; deferred fees debit their original liability account. Refunds draw only available receipt funds. Reversals preserve original journals and dimensions; allocation/receipt/credit/refund reversals need permission and reason. Generic journal reversal cannot bypass billing state. Commands reject dates earlier than existing financial activity, preventing negative historical balances after backdated reversals/reallocations.
- Central `AccountingPostingService.post` owns posting. Required mappings, active accounts/dimensions and open periods are validated; disabled accounting or failed posting blocks and rolls back the entire financial action. Branch, cost center and business unit propagate to every line and reversal.
- Legacy POS Payment tenders remain unchanged. Existing service-invoice writes/refunds must use billing commands; generic sale posting returns the existing service journal and backfill excludes managed service invoices. Shared invoice serialization and receipt printing include service lines.
- Campus/dimension-scoped, paginated outstanding balances and as-of receivables reconciliation compare the same School source population with posted central journals. Receipt advances are separately labeled. No replacement P&L or separate ledger.
- Reused SIS CRUD/schema/detail/permissions/audit and relation fields. Added fee preview/issue, financial transaction, printable receipt and reconciliation screens. Financial actions require School permissions plus shared finance/sales permissions; customer writes also require shared customer permissions. Owner/admin defaults explicitly receive those shared permissions; teachers receive no financial grants.

## Migrations and preservation

Additive migrations: `sales.0011_phase6_billing`, `sales.0012_phase6_adjustment_reversals`, `school.0009_phase6_billing`. No legacy tender backfill: legacy Payment stays authoritative for product/POS invoices; new allocations accept only managed non-stock invoices.
